NorthStar CAD Bank
A full Canadian online banking prototype built with AngularJS & Node.js. It demonstrates authenticated dummy users, CAD account balances, credit products, E-Transfer flows, biometric login controls, support content, and a profile experience.
Mini Case Study

I designed NorthStar around the decisions a Canadian banking user needs to make quickly: checking available balances, understanding credit exposure, completing transfers with confidence, and finding support without breaking the flow.

The interface emphasizes dashboard scanning, CAD-first copy, realistic dummy accounts, and clear transaction states so a recruiter can evaluate both the technical build and the fintech product reasoning behind it.
